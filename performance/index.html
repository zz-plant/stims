Before you start

Compatibility and performance

Stims runs in any browser with WebGL2. It uses WebGPU where the browser supports it, and lowers its own detail when frames start dropping.

Open Stims Browse presets

Best case

A recent laptop or desktop

Heavy presets need a discrete or recent integrated GPU and up-to-date graphics drivers to run at full resolution.

When frames drop

Same preset, less detail

Stims lowers resolution, feedback-buffer size, and particle density. The preset's equations and its response to audio do not change.

First run

Start with demo audio

Play demo needs no microphone or tab permission. Switch to your own audio once a preset is running.

By device

What to expect

Expected behavior by device
Device Expect
Recent desktop or laptop Full resolution on most presets, including the heaviest shaders.
Older laptop or integrated graphics Detail drops on heavy presets to hold the frame rate.
Laptop on battery Capped at 60fps, and at 30fps below 35% charge. Settings can turn this off.
Phone or tablet Starts at a lower quality step. Touch gestures change and steer presets.
TV browser Choose TV balanced in Settings and leave the other options alone.

Automatic

What Stims changes on its own

  • Resolution, feedback-buffer size, and particle density.
  • Detail steps back up once frames have room again.
  • Settings shows whether detail is currently below what you chose.

Manual

If a preset stutters

  • In Settings → Visuals & Display, pick Battery saver.
  • Lower Resolution limit or Particle density under Performance.
  • Set Renderer to WebGL if WebGPU misbehaves on your machine.

One measurement

How performance changes are checked

A code change is benchmarked in a real browser with the preset, audio, viewport, renderer, and quality step held constant. In one equation-heavy WebGPU case on an Apple M1 Max, a JIT change cut average frame work from 17.56 ms to 15.31 ms at 4× CPU throttling, and median FPS went from 58.14 to 59.88.

That is one preset on one machine, not a promise about yours. The method, and the noisier tier that was left out, are in the repository's runtime performance notes .

First steps

Getting started

  1. Open Stims and press Play demo.
  2. Press → or swipe to try other presets.
  3. Then switch to your own audio: a microphone, a file, a tab, or a YouTube link.
