Count the flashes WCAG counts, and stop them while they happen.

Pairs of opposing luminance changes of 10% or more, in any 10-degree visual field, more than three times in any second, plus the PEAT/Harding red-flash criterion. The offline half audits frames you already have. The live half watches a canvas, counts the same flashes as they appear, and solves for the brightness that puts the next one under the limit. Pure TypeScript, no dependencies.

npm install flash-guard Check a video

These are screen-content heuristics, not a medical device. They flag content worth review and reduce measured flash rate; they do not certify anything as safe.

Frames are decoded and analysed in your browser with analyzeRgbaFrames. Nothing is uploaded. Sampling is 30 frames a second at 320 pixels wide, which is what the standard's area and rate tests need.

The rule, as implemented

WCAG 2.3.1 saysflash-guard does
A flash is a pair of opposing changes in relative luminance of 10% or more of maximumA tile transition qualifies when the swing is at least 0.1; a flash is counted when a qualifying field-wide transition reverses the previous one
...where the darker image is below 0.80The darker end of the pair must be under 0.8
The flashing area must exceed 25% of any 10-degree visual fieldA window a third of each axis slides across the tile grid with a summed-area table; the transition counts if any window has 25% qualifying pixels
More than three flashes in any one-second period failsPeak count over any sliding 1 s window, compared against 3
Red flash: a transition to or from a saturated redSaturated when R/(R+G+B) is at least 0.8; qualifies when max(0, R-G-B)·320 changes by more than 20

Magnitude is decided per pixel before any spatial aggregation. Averaging luminance over a tile first scales every swing down by the flashing region's coverage, and on sparse bright-on-black content that pushed real flashes two orders of magnitude under the threshold.

How the governor decides

It engages before the limit

Intervention starts at the second flash in the window, not the fourth. Acting only on the fourth would mean the failing sequence had already been shown.

It solves rather than ramps

On a flash it measures the swing it just observed and computes the scale that puts that swing under the threshold, then jumps there in one step. Blind ramping let a black-to-white strobe land nine flashes before the clamp caught up.

It starts gentle

The first hold is 0.2, so a mild flicker is not punished like a full strobe; the solved step finds the right level.

It releases slowly, after a delay

Release begins once the window has been clear for 1.5 s and eases off at 0.004 per frame. Releasing as soon as the window emptied let the strobe restart and settle into a limit cycle at 8 flashes a second.

It compares frames 16.7 ms apart

On a 120 Hz display, frame-to-frame twinkle in calm content read as 82 flashes a second; compared one 60 Hz frame apart it read 2.4, and real strobes read the same either way.

It reads what the viewer sees

The sample is scaled by the mitigation already in force. Otherwise the governor never observes its own effect and escalates to the ceiling.

Cost of the live sample

The sampler snapshots the canvas inside the draw with createImageBitmap, already downscaled to a 16x16 grid, and hands it to a worker whose readback does the waiting for the GPU. Measured on an M1 Max at 1280x720 in headless Chromium, per sample:

API at a glance

analyzeRgbaFrames(frames, options)RGBA frames in, FlashAnalysis out. createFlashFrameCounter is the streaming form.
analyzeFlashTimeline({ frames, deltaMs, cols, rows })Per-frame luminance grids in, analysis out.
analyzeFlashEvents(input)Per-tile qualifying-pixel counts in, analysis out; the only path that evaluates red flash.
classifyFlashRisk(analysis)none, low, medium or high; high means over the WCAG limit on either channel.
createFlashGovernor(options)The live state machine: one luminance grid per frame in, { hold, luminanceScale, flashesInWindow } out.
createFlashController({ canvas, applyLuminanceScale })Sampler plus governor plus the apply-on-change loop; call tick(now) from your draw.
createBrightnessFilterApplier(element)An applier that writes a CSS brightness() filter.
createFlashSampler({ grid, createWorker })Canvas to luminance grid, off the main thread when possible.
relativeLuminance, peakWindowFraction, the threshold constantsThe primitives every path is built from, defined once.
